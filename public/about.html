About WeatherNow

WeatherNow is your reliable source for accurate, real-time weather updates powered by the OpenWeatherMap API. Our platform helps users track current conditions, plan their week with forecasts, and explore visual weather maps.

Whether you're a developer integrating weather into your app or someone planning a weekend trip, WeatherNow gives you simple, sleek, and trustworthy weather insights.

This site is built with modern web technologies.
